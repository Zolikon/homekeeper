Key design decisions

Future events — full opacity, white background
Active event — teal border, highlighted, "Most" badge inside
Past events — dimmed out, reduced opacity
Flight card — blue tint, links to flight page on tap
Red line — current time indicator, timeline auto-scrolls here on "today"
No endTime — min-height = 30 min (displayed with bottom dashed border to indicate open-ended)
